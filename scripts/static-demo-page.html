Design it here. We print it right.

This page stands in for merchysmarket.com. The Design Studio in the corner is the real widget: pick a service, describe your design, review 6 one-color concepts, then request a vector file, save, or send it to a product.

Try the flow

  1. Choose a service (DTF Printing is a good start).
  2. Enter a name, email, and your business or event. Pick up to 3 styles.
  3. Review the 6 concepts and select your favorites.
  4. Choose Vector File, Save for Later, or Add to Product.
Demo build: concepts are layout previews built from your text, and requests stay on this page. The live version generates AI concepts and routes every request to the Merchy's team.
