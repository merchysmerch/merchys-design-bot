What happens inside

  1. Art director questions, tuned to the product.
  2. Pick any number of styles. One concept per style, arriving one at a time.
  3. Like the ones that work. For each: a few questions, notes, then 5 refinements run in the background. Skip is allowed.
  4. Review board: every style with its original plus 5, numbered. Select, add notes per image, say what you do not want.
  5. Book a call with the artist. Approved designs, notes and dislikes go to the design team and the client's logo library.
Demo build: concepts are full-color layout previews built from the client's answers, and nothing leaves the page. The live version generates AI concepts, posts to Asana, emails the brief, and books through Calendly.
